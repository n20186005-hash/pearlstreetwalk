About Pearl Street Mall

Not a mall. A civic living room.

Welcome to Pearl Street Mall, widely recognized as the central Pearl Street Walk. Located in the heart of Boulder, Colorado, United States, this destination serves as a primary hub for travelers visiting the region.

Pearl Street Mall → Boulder → Colorado → United States

History & Significance of Pearl Street Mall

The four-block pedestrian mall opened as a public park in 1977 after Boulder converted this stretch of Pearl Street from car traffic into a walk-first urban space. Brick paving, gathering nodes, play features, trees and street-scale retail remain the defining experience.

Location & How to Visit Pearl Street Walk in Boulder

Make the bricks easy.

Fees

No admission fee for the pedestrian mall. Shopping, meals and ticketed programs are separate.

Best time

Late morning to early evening for the fullest mix of businesses and street life. Summer evenings are the most animated.

Stay length

1.5–3 hours for browsing and a snack; half a day if you add a sit-down meal, museum or nearby trail.

Accessibility

The city lists accessible sidewalks/paths, benches and drinking fountains. Cross-street grades and crowds can vary.

Detailed transportation

Arrive without the circling.

By bus: Downtown Boulder is served by RTD; the downtown station sits near 14th & Walnut, roughly a block from the mall.

By bike: Boulder’s bike network connects downtown with the Boulder Creek corridor. Use signed bike parking and walk your bike when crowds are dense.

On foot: The core pedestrian mall runs from 11th to 15th Street; Pearl Street continues beyond both ends with more shops and dining.

Parking

Use a city garage first.

Convenient downtown garages include 15th & Pearl, 14th & Walnut, 11th & Walnut and 11th & Spruce. City garage pricing changes, so confirm rates before arrival; weekends and city holidays are often free in downtown city garages, with listed exceptions.

Tip: special events can fill the closest facilities quickly. A garage two or three blocks away is often faster than hunting curb space.

FAQ

Before you go.

Where is Pearl Street Mall located?+

Pearl Street Mall is located at 1303 Pearl St, Boulder, Colorado 80302, United States, near 13th Street in the pedestrian core of downtown.

Is Pearl Street Mall free to visit?+

Yes. The pedestrian mall itself is a public outdoor space with no admission fee. Purchases, dining, performances and ticketed events are separate.

When is the best time to visit?+

Late morning through early evening gives you the broadest mix of open shops, patios and street activity. Summer evenings are lively; shoulder seasons are calmer.

How long should I plan to stay?+

About 1.5–3 hours works well for a first visit, with more time if you plan a meal, shopping, a museum stop or an event.

Where should I park?+

City of Boulder operates multiple downtown garages close to Pearl Street, including 15th & Pearl, 14th & Walnut, 11th & Walnut and 11th & Spruce. Check current city rates before arrival.

Are business hours the same as the mall hours?+

No. The outdoor pedestrian space is accessible at all hours, but every shop, restaurant, visitor service and event sets its own schedule.

What is there to do near Pearl Street Mall?+

Within a short walk you can explore nearby landmarks such as Museum of Boulder and Boulder Creek Path, plus Boulder’s shops, dining and creek-side trails.
